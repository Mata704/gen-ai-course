# Retrieval distractors

These notes deliberately reuse Apollo vocabulary but do not contain the
evidence required to answer the evaluation questions.

## First crewed mission terminology

The expressions first crewed Apollo flight, first crewed lunar orbit and first
crewed lunar landing describe three different milestones. A search based only
on repeated words can confuse them. This terminology note does not identify the
mission or crew associated with any milestone.

## Landing, orbit and lunar module glossary

A crew could remain in lunar orbit while other astronauts walked on the Moon.
A lunar module could support a planned landing or be used during an emergency.
These general descriptions do not identify a mission, a crew member or the
cause of an aborted landing.

## Surface exploration measurements

Later missions used vehicles, travelled longer distances and collected lunar
material. Miles travelled and pounds collected measure different achievements.
This comparison contains no mission names and no authoritative measurements.

## Mission identifiers index

Names such as Surveyor 3, Lunar Roving Vehicle and Apollo are useful exact
identifiers. An index mentioning an identifier is not evidence that answers a
question about who visited it or what happened during that mission.
