# The first Apollo lunar landings

## Apollo 11: first humans on the lunar surface

Apollo 11 launched on July 16, 1969, landed on the Moon on July 20, and
splashed down on July 24. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin became the first
humans to walk on the lunar surface. Michael Collins remained in lunar orbit.

The distinction between the crew roles matters: Armstrong and Aldrin went to
the surface, while Collins stayed in orbit. The mission is the answer to
questions about the first crewed lunar landing, not the first crewed lunar
orbit, which was Apollo 8.

## Apollo 12: a precision destination

Apollo 12 launched on November 14, 1969, landed on November 19, and splashed
down on November 24. It made the Apollo program's second crewed lunar landing.

The crew visited Surveyor 3, a robotic lander that had already been on the Moon
for more than two years. This exact name is useful when testing whether a
retrieval system can find identifiers and proper nouns rather than only broad
semantic similarity.
