# Early Apollo missions

## Apollo 1: a preflight tragedy

Apollo 1 never launched. On January 27, 1967, a fire broke out inside its
command module during a preflight test at Cape Kennedy. Astronauts Gus Grissom,
Ed White, and Roger Chaffee died. Because this happened before launch, Apollo 1
must not be described as an Earth-orbit or lunar mission.

## Apollo 7: the first crewed Apollo flight

Apollo 7 launched on October 11, 1968, and splashed down on October 22. It was
the first crewed mission of the Apollo program. Wally Schirra, Donn Eisele, and
Walt Cunningham spent ten days testing the spacecraft in Earth orbit.

Apollo 7 is therefore relevant to questions about the first crewed Apollo
flight and Earth-orbit testing. It is not the answer to questions about the
first crew to reach the Moon.

## Apollo 8: the first crewed lunar orbit

Apollo 8 launched on December 21, 1968, and returned to Earth on December 27.
Frank Borman, Jim Lovell, and Bill Anders became the first crew to travel to the
Moon and orbit it. The mission did not attempt a lunar landing.

Apollo 8 is the relevant mission when a question asks about the first crewed
journey to the Moon or the first crewed lunar orbit. Apollo 11, not Apollo 8,
made the first crewed lunar landing.
