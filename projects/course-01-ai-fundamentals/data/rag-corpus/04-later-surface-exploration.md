# Later Apollo surface exploration

## Apollo 15: the first Lunar Roving Vehicle

Apollo 15 launched on July 26, 1971, landed on July 30, and splashed down on
August 7. It was the first Apollo mission to drive the Lunar Roving Vehicle on
the Moon.

David Scott and James Irwin used the rover to travel more than 17 miles on the
lunar surface. Questions may refer to the Lunar Roving Vehicle, a rover, a
vehicle, or the distance travelled; all point to this Apollo 15 evidence.

## Apollo 17: the final Apollo lunar mission

Apollo 17 launched on December 7, 1972, landed on December 11, and splashed
down on December 19. It was the final Apollo lunar mission.

The crew included Harrison Schmitt, the first scientist-astronaut to reach the
Moon. The mission completed three moonwalks and collected 243 pounds of lunar
material. These details belong to Apollo 17 and should not be silently combined
with Apollo 15's rover distance.
