# Apollo 13: an aborted landing

## Planned destination and launch

Apollo 13 launched on April 11, 1970. The mission was intended to make a lunar
landing, but that planned landing did not happen.

## Explosion and change of mission

An explosion damaged the spacecraft and ended the lunar-landing objective.
The crew circled the Moon instead of landing. During the emergency, the crew
used the lunar module as a lifeboat.

## Safe return

Apollo 13 splashed down safely on Earth on April 17. A grounded answer should
preserve both sides of the event: the planned landing was aborted, and the crew
returned safely. The source does not claim that the astronauts walked on the
Moon.
